      A NOTE FROM THE CARTOGRAPHER

      An imagined world.
      A different perspective.

      The Known World is an unofficial atlas of the world created by George R. R. Martin. It is a curated view of Westeros and Essos, without character journeys or plot timelines.

      The coastlines are original schematic geometry. Relief is generated for exploration, with approximate place positions; it is not canonically measured topography or a complete fictional planet. No third-party map tiles, textures or artwork are loaded.

      Names and fictional geography belong to their respective rights holders. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. Place panels link to their geographic references.
